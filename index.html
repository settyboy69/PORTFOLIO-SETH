I'm Seth Ongotan, an IT Associate based in San Pedro City, Laguna. I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from SPCBA in August 2026 — and transitioned from intern to full-time associate at Tiger Global Business Services Inc. within the same year.
